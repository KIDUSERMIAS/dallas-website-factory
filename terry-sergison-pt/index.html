About the Practice

Individualized Treatment, Not a Rotation of Patients

Terry W. Sergison, PT is a Garland physical therapy practice built around one-on-one time with a therapist — not a crowded gym floor. Whether you're recovering from surgery, working through an injury, or managing ongoing pain, treatment is built around your specific condition and goals.

Located on Walter Reed Boulevard, the clinic serves Garland and the surrounding communities with hands-on care and a clear, honest approach to cost — self-pay patients know the price up front, and insured patients get their coverage verified before treatment begins.
