About the Shop

A Night Shift Bakery

Most donut shops close by noon — Morning Donuts does the opposite. Doors open at 5 PM and stay open past midnight, with yeast and cake donuts made fresh alongside sausage rolls and croissant sandwiches for anyone working late, studying late, or just craving something sweet after dark.

Located on Belt Line Road in Garland, it's a straightforward, no-frills counter — walk up, order off the board, and go.
