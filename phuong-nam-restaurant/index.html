About the Kitchen

Built on Broth, Herbs, and Patience

Vietnamese noodle soups take time — broths simmered low, rice noodles cooked to order, herbs and lime added fresh at the table. That's the standard behind every bowl at Phương Nam, from a simple chicken hủ tiếu to the bún mắm regulars ask for by name.

Located on West Walnut Street in Garland, Phương Nam is a neighborhood spot for guests who know Vietnamese food and guests trying it for the first time alike.
